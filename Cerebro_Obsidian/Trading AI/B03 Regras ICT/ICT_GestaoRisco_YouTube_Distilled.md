## Risk Management Rules

### Entry Conditions:
- Do not enter trades if no fair value Gap allows for a four-point stop loss.
- When trading short, set a limit order to sell inside the sell-side of balance buyid and efficiency.
- Respect the fair value gap and wait for price to retrace back up before entering a long position.

### Invalidation:
- If price moves above the first candle in the CI on a two-minute chart, consider closing the trade.
- Stop losses should be placed below the low of a bearish order block or at the bottom of a swing low.
- Do not place stop losses inside a fair value Gap immediately after a shift in market structure.
- If price moves above recent swing highs while trailing a stop loss, it may indicate a false signal or setup failure.

### Targets / Objectives:
- Aim for relative equal lows or high as targets.
- Use FIB extension confluences to reach for profit targets while trading long positions.

## Trading Psychology Rules

### Entry Conditions:
- Focus on trading during specific times, such as 10 AM to 11 AM for certain setups.
- Utilize the New York open as a key time frame for trade entries and exits.
- Look for setups where price retracts to specific levels, such as old highs or lows, before entering trades.

### Invalidation:
- Be cautious of placing stop losses in areas with high liquidity, such as below relative equal lows or within inefficiencies.
- Avoid placing stop losses inside displacement legs or inefficiencies after significant price movements.

### Key Concepts
- Maintain discipline and avoid over-leveraging or over-trading to prevent account blow-up.
- Use realistic objectives that are within reach based on the market's current conditions.
- Stay patient and let trades play out naturally without making unnecessary adjustments or exits.
- Journal entries can provide insights into your trading psychology, helping to identify biases and improve decision-making.
