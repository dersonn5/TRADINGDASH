## Entry Conditions

- Enter short during London open or New York open kill zones in a bearish market environment, targeting retracement lower into the bearish order block.
- Enter long during London open or New York open kill zones in a bullish market environment, aiming for buy side liquidity above previous day's high or intra-week high inside the 20-day IA data range.
- Enter trades when price retraces back into an order block after breaking it.
- Hunt long opportunities when the weekly candle is likely to expand higher.

## Invalidation

- Break below a bearish order block invalidates the setup for short positions.
- Break above a bullish order block invalidates the setup for long positions.
- A break below or above a fair value gap without respecting it signals invalidation of the trade setup.
- Displacement and subsequent break below short-term levels indicates invalidation of the setup.

## Targets / Objectives

- Bearish Target: Retrace lower into the bearish order block, aiming for the low of the weekly range.
- Bullish Target: Rally up to the high of the week, targeting external range liquidity using kill zones or standard deviations and PD convergence minus five Pips as entry price when selling.

## Key Concepts

- **Fair Value Gap**: Use fair value gaps to identify potential areas where price is expected to trade next. Enter short when price trades up into a fair value gap after breaking below an old swing low.
- **Order Blocks**: Identify order blocks and use them to define liquidity zones for entry points and targets.
- **Market Structure Shifts**: Utilize weekly and monthly equal highs/lows to identify market structure shifts, looking for relative equal highs or lows in the context of order flow.
- **PD Array Considerations**: Frame trades based on external range liquidity using PD arrays, confirming setups with inter-market analysis.
