## Description
How to identify, use, and invalidate Order Blocks and Breakers

## Entry Conditions

- **Bullish Order Block**: Buy on a stop if trading above an old low or below an old high. Use limit orders to enter trades at a bullish order block.
- Look for a bullish order block or rejection block on the daily chart, nested within weekly discount arrays.
- In bull markets, buy all daily bullish discount arrays including order blocks, breakers, fair value gaps, liquidity voids, and old lows/highs.

- **Bearish Order Block**: Sell short on a stop if trading below an old high or above an old low. Use limit orders to enter trades at a bearish order block.
- Look for bearish order blocks when price rallies into them.
- In bear markets, look for bearish order blocks as potential selling opportunities.
- Enter short positions on the close of a down candle that breaks lower after a retracement higher.

## Invalidation

- **Bullish Order Block**: Invalidated by price breaking through the old high or if price rallies above the mean threshold of the order block on lower timeframes.
- **Bearish Order Block**: Invalidated by price breaking through the old low or if price breaks below the mean threshold of the order block on lower timeframes.

## Targets/Objectives

- **Bullish Order Block**: Target higher premium arrays such as mitigation blocks, breakers, liquidity voids, fair value gaps, and order blocks above the current market action.
- Use the midpoint or consequent encroachment as the ideal premium PD array to expect higher data form.
- Trade up to the high or resistance levels near the bearish order block for profit targets.

- **Bearish Order Block**: Target lower discount arrays such as rejection blocks, old highs, and bearish order blocks below the current market action.
- Use the fair value gap as a target after breaking it.
- Target levels defined by old highs, old lows, rejection blocks, and bearish order blocks in consolidation ranges.

## Key Concepts

- **Bullish Order Block**: Formed when price closes above them.
- **Bearish Order Block**: Formed when price closes below them.
- **Breaker Blocks**:
  - Bullish breaker blocks are formed by a close above the level.
  - Bearish breaker blocks are formed by a close below the level.
