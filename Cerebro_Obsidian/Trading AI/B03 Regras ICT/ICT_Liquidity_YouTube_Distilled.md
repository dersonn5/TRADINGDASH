## Entry Conditions
- In a stage one or two reaccumulation, when price moves into a 15-minute or five-minute discount fair value Gap PD array that converges with a standard deviation of no more than -3 during London open or New York open.
- When in a market maker sell model, target the buy side liquidity above the old high.
- Trade up into bearish fair value gaps.
- Enter short when price rallies into bearish order blocks.
- Enter short on retracements back down into old lows or bearish order blocks after a rally.

## Invalidation
- If price moves above the premium fair value Gap PD array during London open or New York open (Bullish Market).
- If price moves below the discount fair value Gap PD array during London open or New York open (Bearish Market).
- Prices breaking through a rejection block above the candle's body invalidates the setup.
- Break lower through the low of the bearish order block.
- Close below the relative equal lows that act as support.

## Targets & Objectives
- Target the buy side liquidity above the previous day’s high (Bullish Market).
- Aim for a premium array at least 40 to 50 Pips away from the low (Bullish Market).
- Target the sell side liquidity below the old lows (Bearish Market).
- Aim for a discount array at least 40 to 50 Pips away from the high (Bearish Market).
- Target areas include old highs, old lows, and fair value gaps.
- Look for price action that reaches up from discount levels to premium levels.
- Use the 5-day average daily range high as a target for long trades.

## Key Concepts
- Focus on bearish order blocks, liquidity voids, and fair value gaps in bearish markets.
- Identify sell side liquidity resting below old lows or discount fair value gaps.
- Look for buy side liquidity above old highs or premium fair value gaps.
- Fair value gaps act as key draw points and targets.
- Bearish order blocks provide optimal trade entries on short positions.
- Relative equal highs and lows indicate potential resistance or support levels for liquidity sweep.
