## Entry Conditions
- Bullish: Price moves down into a 15 or five-minute discount Fair Value Gap PD that converges with a standard deviation of no more than -3 standard deviations during London open or New York open.
- Bearish: Price moves up into a 15-minute premium Fair Value Gap PD array that converges with a standard deviation of no more than +3 standard deviations during London open or New York open.
- Look for price to reach up from a discount level seen on the weekly chart towards premium PDF arrays when higher time frames are bullish.
- When the market opens, if it rallies into a bearish order block and then aggressively runs for sell-side liquidity below that level, consider selling short.

## Invalidation
- Bullish: Reversal out of the Fair Value Gap PD array.
- Bearish: Reversal up into the Fair Value Gap PD array.
- A move through a bearish order block invalidates the setup.
- If price trades below the midpoint of the down closed candle forming the fair value gap, invalidate the setup.
- If price trades back down to the low of the weekly chart, invalidate the setup.

## Targets & Objectives
- Bullish: Target the buy side liquidity above the previous day's high or intra-week high inside the 20-day IA data range.
- Bearish: Target the sell side liquidity below the old lows.
- Use the 5-day average daily Range High as a target for long trades in the New York Kill Zone.
- Expect price to expand from the fair value gap towards the low of the weekly chart.
- The market will likely run from the fair value gap to the low of the hourly chart.

## Key Concepts
- Fair Value Gap (FVG): Trade back into a Fair Value Gap when it acts as support on the daily chart, especially if higher time frames are bullish. Look for price to reach up from a discount level seen on the weekly chart towards premium PDF arrays.
- Imbalance: Use relative equal lows and highs to identify imbalances. A trade setup is invalidated if price trades below or above these levels, respectively. Imbalance can be identified by a shift in market structure where price does not pierce through expected support or resistance levels.
