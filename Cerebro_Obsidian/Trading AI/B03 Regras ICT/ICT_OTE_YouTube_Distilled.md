## Targets & Objectives

- **Bullish Market:**
  - Target buy side liquidity above the previous day's high or intra-week high inside the 20-day IA data range.

- **Bearish Market:**

## Key Concepts

- **Fair Value Gaps**: Use weekly fair value gaps as support or resistance levels for trade setups. Consider the next logical discount or premium array based on standard deviation ranges during London open and New York open.
